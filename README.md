# IT Audit Toolkit

## Overview

Welcome to my IT Audit Toolkit.

This repository contains the templates, checklists, questionnaires, working papers, and reporting documents I use to conduct structured IT audit and cybersecurity assessments. It has been developed as part of my professional portfolio and is based on industry-recognized governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) practices.

The purpose of this repository is to demonstrate a structured approach to planning, executing, documenting, and reporting IT audits while continuously expanding my practical experience.

---

## Objectives

- Conduct structured IT audit engagements
- Evaluate cybersecurity risks and security controls
- Document audit evidence and observations
- Produce professional audit reports
- Support governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) initiatives

---

## Frameworks Referenced

- NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0
- CIS Critical Security Controls
- OWASP Top 10
- ISO/IEC 27001 (where applicable)

---

## Repository Structure

 Audit Planning

Templates used to define audit scope, objectives, timelines, and engagement planning.

 Client Questionnaires

Standardized questionnaires used to gather information about an organization's technology environment, business processes, and security practices.

 Audit Checklists

Assessment checklists aligned with recognized IT audit and cybersecurity frameworks.

 Risk Assessment

Risk registers, risk matrices, and documentation used to identify, evaluate, and prioritize risks.

 Evidence Collection

Templates used to document walkthroughs, interviews, screenshots, and supporting audit evidence.

 Reporting

Professional templates for executive summaries, audit findings, remediation plans, and final audit reports.
